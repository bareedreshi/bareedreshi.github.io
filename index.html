Selected work

Google Business Profile visibility recovery

Symptom

Maps visibility across the retail footprint collapsed year over year.

Assumed

Content or links. It was neither.

Root cause

Location profile completeness sitting well below the threshold, compounded by local algorithm changes that had quietly raised the bar for what counts as complete.

Action

Remediation across every Arizona location, plus a structured review and reputation program to hold the gains.

Lesson

In multi-location local search, the boring data-hygiene problem beats the interesting strategy problem most of the time.

Local service business — zero web presence to live and indexed

Client

A mobile automotive locksmith serving the Phoenix metro. Strong Google Business Profile, steady review volume, and no website at all — invisible outside the map pack.

Built

A mobile-first single-page site from scratch: full LocalBusiness schema, geo meta and coordinates, service-area coverage across Phoenix, Mesa, Tempe, Scottsdale, Chandler, Peoria, Avondale and Goodyear, and copy written for emergency intent rather than brand browsing.

Reasoning

Someone locked out of their car at 9pm does not read an About page. Every decision — single page, phone number fixed in view, service list above the fold, no forms — came from that one behavioural fact.

Multi-brand schema and entity program

Problem

Three brands with different entity types under one ownership group: retail locations, family entertainment venues, and a nonprofit education organization. Different schema requirements, different local footprints, one marketing function.

Action

Deployed Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQ, and Course markup across all three, holding entity consistency across Google Business Profile, on-site markup, and content.

Why

Entity consistency is what lets each brand resolve as itself in local results and AI-generated answers, rather than as a blur of the parent group.

Conversion tracking rebuild

Symptom

Automated bidding optimizing against a conversion foundation full of duplicate and junk actions.

Action

Audited and cleared it, then assigned defensible business values to offline outcomes — store visits, direction requests, phone calls — so Smart Bidding optimized toward real business results rather than surface conversions.

Side effect

It made organic performance measurable in the same frame as paid, which is what finally let search work get argued for on equal footing at budget time.
